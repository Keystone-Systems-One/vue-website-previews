# THE VUE website previews

Interactive client-review drafts for After Dark and Weddings at THE VUE.

- [After Dark](https://keystone-systems-one.github.io/vue-website-previews/after-dark.html)
- [Weddings](https://keystone-systems-one.github.io/vue-website-previews/the-occasion.html)
- [Both website previews](https://keystone-systems-one.github.io/vue-website-previews/)

Sample events, dates and prices are illustrative. Forms create local previews only; they do not send messages or reserve dates. Ticket interactions are demonstrations and do not collect payments. Final venue content and services remain subject to approval.

Real venue imagery and labeled stock inspiration are preserved. See [asset attribution](assets/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

This repository contains only the public website previews. Business plans, questionnaires, responses, research and internal client documents are excluded.
