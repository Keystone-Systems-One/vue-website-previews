A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

What should we include in our enquiry?+

Your event type, preferred date, estimated guest count, and any ideas for food, music or the atmosphere.

Can we customise the setup?+

Share your preferred setup in the enquiry. Actual layouts, furnishings, equipment and available services must be confirmed with the venue.

Are food and drinks included?+

Menu choices, catering arrangements, bar service and pricing are to be confirmed. No package or inclusion is represented by this demo.

Is the venue accessible?+

Ask the venue to confirm entrances, bathrooms, seating arrangements and any specific access requirements before booking.
