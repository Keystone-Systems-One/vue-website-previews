A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

Can we arrange a tour?+

Use the enquiry form and choose “Arrange a venue tour.” This demo prepares an enquiry preview; a real tour will need confirmation from the venue.

What is included in a wedding booking?+

Packages, catering, drinks, décor, setup and staffing need to be confirmed with VUE ON MAIN. This concept does not promise an unverified package.

How many guests can we invite?+

The venue’s actual seated and standing capacities have not been confirmed. Include your estimated guest count in your enquiry so the team can discuss a suitable arrangement.

Can we bring our own vendors?+

Catering, music, photography and other vendor policies should be confirmed with the venue before you make arrangements.

How do we secure a date?+

Start an enquiry with your preferred date. This demo cannot confirm availability or take a deposit.
